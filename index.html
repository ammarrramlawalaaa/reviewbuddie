FAQ

Questions sellers ask us

Is rewarding reviews allowed on marketplaces?

ReviewBuddie rewards customers for taking part in feedback, never for a star rating. Every customer sees the same flow and keeps the reward whatever they write, so there is no review gating. Always check your marketplace's current policy for your category and region. Our Compliance Center keeps the rules in one place.

How do customers reach the claim page?

Each campaign creates a print-ready QR code for your package insert and a shareable link for WhatsApp, email, SMS or your Instagram bio. The customer scans, picks where they bought, writes a review and claims the reward. No app or account is needed.

Which marketplaces are supported?

20+ marketplaces including Amazon (22 regions), Flipkart, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, Target, Myntra, Nykaa, Firstcry, Meesho, TEMU, Google Business and Trustpilot. One product can be linked to several marketplaces.

What rewards can I offer?

Eight promotion types: warranty registration, extended warranty, cashback, coupon code, gift card, digital download, product information and product giveaway, plus your own custom reward. Each gets a branded, animated claim card.

Is the AI review assistant writing fake reviews?

No. The customer taps tags that describe their own experience, the assistant turns those into a draft in their language, and the customer edits and posts it themselves. It helps honest buyers put their experience into words.

What does the free plan include?

Every feature, every marketplace and unlimited products, capped at 10 reviews and 10 leads a month. No credit card, free forever.

How long does setup take?

Most sellers go live in under 5 minutes: add a product by link, pick a reward, and download the QR insert.
