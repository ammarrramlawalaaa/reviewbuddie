How it works

How do I get more reviews on Amazon India without breaking the rules?

Remove the hurdles. Ask at the right time. Let customers speak in their own words.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Add the QR insert to your box

    Add your product in ReviewBuddie and print the QR insert. It goes in the box with every order. Nothing changes in how you pack or ship.

    Video placeholderStep 1 video

    Seller prints the QR insert and drops it into a product box. Caption: "Print once. Pack as usual."

  2. 2

    Step 2: Customer registers warranty and watches your guide

    They scan with their phone camera. No app, no login. They register their warranty and see your setup video or care guide.

    Video placeholderStep 2 video

    Customer scans the QR, fills a 3-field warranty form, then plays a how-to video. Caption: "Warranty registered."

    9:16 phone screen · 6–8s loop · ≤600KB
  3. 3

    Step 3: A timely, compliant review request

    Once they've had time to use the product, they get one neutral request to share their experience. If writing feels hard, AI helps them put their own rating and points into words. They edit it and post it themselves.

    Days later: a WhatsApp-style message asks how the product is. Customer picks their own rating, taps a few points, AI drafts it, customer edits and taps "Post on Amazon".

FAQ

Questions sellers ask before they start

Is this allowed by Amazon?

ReviewBuddie is built around Amazon's customer review rules. Every customer gets the same neutral request, nobody is offered anything in return, and we never filter who gets asked. Marketplace policies change, so we keep our flows up to date, and we suggest you also check the latest guidelines in Seller Central.

Do you give customers incentives for reviews?

No, never. No discounts, gifts, cashback or free products in exchange for a review. Warranty registration and product guides are part of the product experience, and customers get them whether or not they ever leave a review.

Do customers need an app?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera and everything opens in the browser. No app download, no login.

Which marketplaces do you support?

Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa and FirstCry. The review request takes customers straight to your product's review page on the marketplace they bought from.

How much does it cost?

ReviewBuddie is free for founding sellers during early access. We'll share pricing well before launch, and you'll never be charged without agreeing to it first.

How long does setup take?

Add your product, upload your guides, print the QR insert and put it in your boxes. Founding sellers get personal setup help from our team on WhatsApp.

Is customer data safe?

Customer details collected through warranty registration belong to you. We don't sell or share them with anyone, and customers can ask for their data to be deleted at any time.

Can I see a demo?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll walk you through it on a short call.
