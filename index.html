FAQ

Questions sellers ask us

Is rewarding reviews allowed on marketplaces?

ReviewBuddie rewards customers for honest feedback. The reward is never conditional on a star rating, and customers keep the reward regardless of what they write. Always review your marketplace’s current policies for your category and region; the Compliance Center has guides for Amazon, FTC rules, insert cards and incentives.

How do customers find the claim page?

Through the QR insert in your package, or the campaign link you share on WhatsApp, email, SMS, your Instagram bio or order messages. It opens in the phone browser. No app, no account.

Which marketplaces are supported?

20+ marketplaces, including Amazon in 22 regions, Flipkart, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, Myntra, Nykaa, Google Business and Trustpilot. Customers are routed straight to the right review page.

What rewards can I offer?

Warranty, extended warranty, cashback, coupon code, gift card, digital download, product information and product giveaway, or create your own. Claim rules can ask for a review with a screenshot, a rating only, or nothing at all.

What does the free plan include?

10 reviews and 10 leads and redirections per month, with every feature included. No credit card needed.

Do I approve rewards manually?

Yes. Claims land in the Claim Center with the customer’s feedback in view. Approve, reject or bulk-deliver; duplicates get an amber fraud flag. Warranty claims can auto-approve.

How is cashback paid out?

On local rails: UPI, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm and Amazon Pay in India, PayPal and Venmo in the US, SEPA in the EU, Interac in Canada and PayID in Australia.
